About the School

Where little minds become big thinkers.

Holy International School provides a warm, disciplined and stimulating environment for children during their most important formative years.

Our approach combines academic foundations with activities, creativity, communication and practical experiences. We believe that education becomes powerful when children are encouraged to ask questions, participate and express themselves.

Every child deserves attention, encouragement and opportunities to discover what they can do.

Strong Foundations Building essential academic skills.
Creative Learning Encouraging curiosity and imagination.
Confidence Helping children express themselves.
Character Growing responsible young citizens.

Message from the Principal's Desk
