What we value

  • Strong subject knowledge and a genuine interest in children's learning.
  • Professional communication, punctuality and responsible classroom practice.
  • Patience, empathy and a positive approach to student development.
  • Willingness to collaborate with colleagues and participate in school activities.

Application essentials

  • Updated CV or résumé with education and relevant experience.
  • Academic and professional qualification details.
  • Recent contact information and the position you are interested in.
  • Relevant teaching, activity or portfolio information, where applicable.
